# Proposal for the owner: the agenda becomes a schedule (P49)

Status: **proposal, waiting for the owner's yes.** Nothing here is built. The agenda's fixes are built (CHANGES section
199): it covers today and the six days after, and names the days it looked at when it is empty.

## The proposal

1. **A day at a time, as a schedule.** A heading per day ("Today · Mon, Oct 26"), entries by start time down the
   column, each row showing its time range, title and place. All-day entries sit apart at the top of their day.
2. **Moving through days.** Arrows move back and forward a day, and Shift (or a long press on a phone) moves a week. A
   "Today" button returns. The keyboard's left and right arrows do the same when the schedule has focus, never while
   the cursor is in a field.
3. **Every entry opens its page**: its time, place, who's coming, its notes and the to-dos linked to it.
4. **Where an entry comes from is shown without color alone**: a small label per row ("Work calendar", "Home
   calendar", "From your notes"), plus an optional color per calendar that the person picks in Settings.
5. **Clashes and lead time stay where they belong.** A clash shows as a line on Today, not as overlapping boxes. A
   meeting to set up shows its lead-time line on Today (CHANGES 199).
6. **On a phone**, one day fills the screen and a swipe moves between days. On a wide window, today and the next two
   days sit side by side, and the window never scrolls sideways.

## Choices for the owner

- **Where it lives**: Today's agenda card becomes the first day of the schedule, with "Open the schedule" leading to a
  full screen (proposed), or the schedule replaces the card on Today.
- **Days side by side on a wide window**: three (proposed), or one.
- **Week view**: not proposed for now (the agenda is for the days ahead); say if wanted.

If the owner says yes, design 7 is updated first, then the screen is built with its browser test at several widths.
